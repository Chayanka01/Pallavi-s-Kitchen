Chicken alu lal jhol (inspired by bong eats)

First Marination of chicken

  • Take 500g curry cut chicken and wash it well
  • Add salt, 200g dahi, 1tbsp black pepper powder, 1 tsp haldi powder and 1 tsp chilli powder to the chicken
  • Let it marinate for at least half an hour
  • After half an hour take 4 to 5 tbsp of the marinade (the dahi mixture) and keep it aside in a separate bowl

Second marination of chicken

  • Remove the chicken from the first marinade and wash it well
  • Drain the chicken and add 2 tsp salt, 2 tsp haldi powder and 2tbsp mustard oil and let it marinate for 15-20 minutes
  • Throw the rest of the first marinade away (REMEMBER TO RESERVE 4 TO 5 TBSP OF THE FIRST DAHI MARINADE)

Birista

  • Thinly slice 2 medium onions
  • Fry in 6-7 tbsp mustard oil heated in a kadhai till golden brown
  • Take the onions out of the oil and keep it in a bowl

Potato

  • Peel 2 large potatoes and cut into quarters (if potatoes are small, cut into halves)
  • Boil the potatoes in salted water till fully cooked
  • Drain and wash with cold water and dry fully
  • Fry the boiled potatoes in the onion frying oil in the kadhai on medium high (900 induction) till a light golden crispy layer develops on top
  • Remove from oil and keep aside

Garam masala powder

  • Dry roast 3 sticks of dalchini, 7 to 8 elaichi and 15-16 laung
  • Cool down and grind to a fine powder

Masala Paste

  • Soak 5-6 dried red chillies in hot water for 10-15 minutes
  • Take out the chillies and make a paste with 12-15 garlic cloves, 1 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder and 1 inch knob of ginger

Note- While making the paste , a bit of chilli soaking water can be used

Curry

  • In the remaining onion potato frying oil in the kadhai, add 1 to 2 tbsp more mustard oil
  • Add 1 tsp sugar and let it caramelize for 2 minutes on medium high (900 induction)
  • Add the masala paste and fry at low medium (600 induction) till the oil separates and raw smell of ginger garlic goes away

Note- Splash some water in between if the paste starts sticking to the kadhai

  • Add 2 tejpatta (bay leaves) and 1 large onion thinly sliced to the kadhai and mix with the masala
  • Cover and cook at low medium (600 induction) for 8-10 minutes till the onions are soft and masala releases oil
  • Add the reserved first marinade (the dahi mixture) to the kadhai and mix well
  • Cover and cook at low medium (600 induction) till the raw smell of dahi goes away and oil is released on top
  • Add the marinated chicken and 10-12 whole garlic cloves
  • Increase the heat to medium high (900 induction) and mix well
  • Cover and add some weight on the cover (like roti belne wala chakla) and cook for 8-10 minutes
  • Open the lid and stir everything, add a splash of water if masala is sticking
  • Cover again and cook at low medium (600 induction) for 20-30 minutes or till the chicken is fully cooked and has developed some roasted brown spots

Note- Stir every 5-10 minutes in between to prevent burning and add a splash of water whenever the masala looks too dry

  • When chicken is fully cooked , add 2 cups of boiling hot water
  • Cover and cook for 5 minutes on high heat (1200 induction) and the oil should float to the top and the gravy should be bubbling and a bit reduced
  • Adjust the salt and add the boiled fried potatoes
  • Add 1 tbsp Garam masala powder, 2 whole green chillies, 1/2 tsp sugar, the Birista and 2 tbsp raw mustard oil
  • Cover and turn the heat off immediately.
  • Keep covered for at least 10 minutes before serving
Background

Lal jhol, red curry, takes its colour from the oil. The chilli paste is fried in mustard oil until the fat separates, and when water goes in the emulsion breaks and red oil rises to the top.

The Bong Eats murgir lal jhol this starts from is spare: the chicken marinated in only turmeric and salt, very little onion, the whole spices ground fresh and added at the end. This version builds on it considerably. There are two marinades, the first in dahi, most of it washed off and a few spoonfuls kept back for the gravy. Sugar is caramelised in the oil before the paste goes in, whole garlic cloves cook with the chicken, and birista, onion fried slowly until deep brown, goes in at the finish with the garam masala. The weighted lid is the same one used for the Chicken Champaran Kosha.
