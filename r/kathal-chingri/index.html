Kathal chingri tricks

Dry roasted Masala powder

  • 5 dry red chillies
  • 6 elaichi
  • 3-4 small pieces dalchini
  • 7-8 laung
  • 1 tbsp jeera
  • 1 tbsp dhaniya seed
  • 5-6 kabab chini
  • black pepper 1 tbsp

Chingri (200g)

  • marinate with salt and haldi powder
  • fry in mustard oil

Kathal (250g)

  • boil kathal and little bit alu with salt, garam masala powder (elaichi loung dalchini) and 5-6 garlic cloves till soft
  • drain and fry till crispy brown bits form
  • keep aside

Gravy

  • mustard oil, tejpatta, jeera, 2 elaichi, 1 small dalchini, 4-5 loung
  • chopped onion, fry a bit, add 1/2 tsp kashmiri mirch powder, fry well
  • add ginger garlic green chilli paste (more garlic than ginger) and fry till raw smell gone, add splash of water if needed
  • add tomato paste (2 tomato) and roasted masala powder 1tsp , cook well
  • add 2 tbsp dahi and mix well, cook till oil separates
  • add fried kathal alu and chingri, mix well , cook for 5 min
  • add very little water, koshao it
  • add ghee, roasted masala powder, sugar, salt, little bit dhaniya
  • done
Background

Unripe jackfruit is cooked as a vegetable across eastern India. The same fruit, left on the tree to ripen, is eaten raw as a sweet.

Boiling the jackfruit with salt and garlic before frying it firms the pieces enough to survive a heavy gravy. Prawns are the usual pairing in fish-eating households; the meatless echorer dalna is more widely known.
