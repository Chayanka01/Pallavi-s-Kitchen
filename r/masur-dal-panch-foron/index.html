Masur dal panch foron diye

  • Heat mustard oil in a pan
  • Add 1 small tejpatta, 2 dried red chillies, 1 spring curry leaves, 1/2 tsp radhuni, 1/4 tsp methi, 1/2 tsp kaala jeera, 1/3 tsp jeera and 1/3 tsp saunf
  • Add 1 small onion finely chopped and 5-6 cloves of garlic finely chopped
  • Add salt and a bit of haldi powder
  • After the onions get golden brown add 1/2 katori soaked masur dal along with 2 katori water
  • boil the dal till it's fully cooked and slightly falls apart
  • Turn off the flame and top with 1 tsp raw mustard oil
Background

The five seeds in the tempering are the five of Bengali panch phoron, with radhuni standing in for black mustard: methi, kala jeera, jeera, saunf and radhuni. Kala jeera here is nigella, the seed Bengali calls kalo jeere, as the Sheem Alu Mishtikumro Posto recipe spells out. In Hindi the same name usually refers to a different seed, black cumin. Curry leaves are less common in Bengali cooking than further south.

Raw mustard oil poured over at the end is a Bengali finishing step. Unheated, the oil keeps the sharp pungency that frying drives off.
