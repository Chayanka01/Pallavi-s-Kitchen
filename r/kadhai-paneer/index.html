Munu style Kadhai Paneer recipe

Pepper garam masala powder

  • Dry roast 1.5 tbsp black pepper, 4 green elaichi, 1/2 inch small stick of dalchini, 1 big blade javitri, 8-10 laung and 1/2 tsp kababchini
  • Cool and grind to a fine powder

Kaju Tomato paste

  • In a pan lightly dry roast 1 tbsp cashew and 1 tbsp charmagaz (melon seeds)
  • Cool and grind to a powder
  • Add 2 green chillies , 1/4th knob of ginger and 1 large tomato to the cashew - charmagaz powder and grind to a smooth paste by adding some water

Paneer

  • Cut 200g paneer into cubes and coat with a pinch of salt and black pepper powder
  • Heat up 5-6 tbsp refined oil in a kadhai and fry the paneer cubes at medium (600 induction) till two faces are browned

NOTE - Keep 4 faces of the cube un-fried for better absorption of gravy

Gravy

  • Take out 2 tbsp of the paneer frying oil and keep for later use
  • Heat up the remaining oil, add 3 green elaichi, 1/2 inch dalchini stick, 1/2 blade javitri and 1 large bay leaf
  • Add 1 small onion finely chopped and fry till it's slightly brown
  • Add the Kaju tomato paste , a pinch of salt and 1/2 tsp sugar
  • Cover and cook with stirring in between on medium (600 induction) till the masala becomes grainy , the raw smell of ginger goes away and the oil floats on top
  • Lower heat to low medium (400 induction) and add 100g whisked dahi and mix well
  • Cover and cook at medium (600 induction) till the dahi releases oil and raw smell goes away
  • Add 1 tsp of the pepper garam masala powder and 1 tsp kasuri methi
  • Add 1/2 glass water and the fried paneer
  • Cover and boil at high heat (1400 induction) till the gravy is reduced to desired thickness
  • Turn off the heat and prepare the vegetable tadka

Vegetable Tadka

  • In a small saucepan add the 2 tbsp reserved paneer frying oil
  • Add 1/2 capsicum, 1 medium onion and 1 medium tomato (seeds removed) , all cut into large chunks
  • Fry at medium high (900 induction) till the vegetables are cooked slightly and their surfaces are a bit charred
  • Add 1 tsp kasuri methi and immediately remove from heat

Final assembly

  • Put the Vegetable tadka in the prepared gravy
  • Mix and immediately cover with a lid and boil the gravy at medium high (900 induction for 5 minutes)
  • Garnish with 2 tsp ghee, 1 whole green chilli, 1/2 tsp sugar and 1/2 tsp pepper garam masala powder
  • Serve
Background

The garam masala here is built mostly on black pepper, with only a little elaichi, dalchini, javitri, laung and kababchini around it, and it goes in twice: once into the gravy and again at the end. The gravy is thickened with cashew and charmagaz, melon seeds, roasted and ground with the tomato.

The paneer is browned on only two of its six faces, leaving the rest soft to take up the gravy. The capsicum, onion and tomato are charred separately in the oil kept back from frying the paneer and go in at the very end, under a lid, so they stay in large pieces.
