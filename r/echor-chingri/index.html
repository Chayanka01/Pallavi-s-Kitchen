Echor chingri Recipe-

  • bhaja masala - roast black pepper, jeera, dhaniya seeds, red chillis and powder
  • boil water with salt, mirchi powder,haldi. Boil echor and peeled cut potato in that
  • cool the boiled vegetables
  • marinate prawns with salt haldi
  • heat mustard oil and fry the boiled vegetables till crispy and brownish
  • heat some more mustard oil and fry the prawns and remove
  • in the same oil add elaichi, lawng , dalchini, tejpatta, little jeera
  • add onion paste and fry well
  • add ginger garlic green chilli paste and fry well. Add some salt
  • add tomato paste and fry well
  • add red chilli powder, jeera powder, dhaniya powder
  • fry well
  • add the fried vegetables and a bit of water , cover and cook for 5 mins till some oil releases
  • add the bhaja masala from above and (a bit of homemade bongeats bhaja masala+ garam masala powder optional)
  • add some more water and reduce it to consistency you like
  • add some ghee and sugar, cover , turn off flame and serve
Background

Echor is green, unripe jackfruit. Boiling it in salted water with turmeric and chilli, then frying it until the edges brown, keeps it from breaking down once it goes into the gravy.

The bhaja masala is black pepper, cumin, coriander seed and red chilli, dry- roasted and ground. It is added at the end rather than during cooking, which leaves the aromatics intact at the table instead of driving them off over the heat.
