The two Urad cells are the other way round in the drawing. The values above are the corrected ones: urad with the skin on is black, and skinned urad is white.

Background

Most dals are sold in two forms, with the skin on (chhilka) and without. Skinned and split dals cook faster and break down to a smooth consistency; those with the skin on hold their shape and take longer, and behave differently enough that they are not always interchangeable.

Dry, the two forms differ mainly in colour. The chart records that difference.
