Bengali street style egg chowmein

Noodles

  • Boil noodles (80g raw weight) in water with some salt a minute or two lesser than the time mentioned on package
  • Drain and wash with cold water
  • Keep in the strainer only and add 1 tsp oil and coat well

Egg

  • In a wide kadhai , heat up 3 tbsp refined oil at medium high (900 induction)
  • Crack one egg directly, add 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Scramble it well
  • Take the eggs out and keep aside

Cooking

  • In the egg frying oil, add 2 more tbsp of refined oil
  • Add 1 large onion thinly sliced and 1/2 medium capsicum thinly sliced
  • Fry for 4-5 minutes on medium high (900 induction)
  • Add 8-10 cloves garlic (finely chopped/smashed) and 2 chopped green chillies
  • Fry for 1-2 minutes
  • Bring heat down to low (400 induction) and add 1/2 tsp black pepper powder, 1 tsp red chilli powder, 1 chicken bouillon cube (optional) , 1/2 tsp MSG, 1 tsp garlic powder and 1 tsp Sunrise Shahi Garam masala powder
  • Add the boiled noodles and scrambled eggs
  • Mix well and add 1.5 tsp soya sauce and 1 tsp vinegar
  • Mix everything well and turn off heat
  • Garnish - Before serving add 2 tbsp thinly sliced raw onions, 2 tbsp thin matchsticks of raw and peeled cucumber, 1 tbsp ketchup and 1/2 tbsp green chilli sauce
  • Serve
Background

The noodles are boiled short of the packet time, rinsed cold and oiled in the strainer so they stay separate. The spices go in on low heat just before the noodles, and soy sauce and vinegar only once everything is in the kadhai. The finish is raw: sliced onion, cucumber matchsticks, ketchup and green chilli sauce on top, added just before serving.

The Egg Vegetable Chowmein instead coats its noodles in a garlic chilli oil before they reach the kadhai.
