Macher Kalia recipe

  • marinate fish (500g rui/katla) with salt haldi, fry till bit crisp in mustard oil
  • boil long cut potatoes till soft
  • cool and fry in oil till slight layer forms
  • make paste of( hot water soaked 5 dried red chillies, soaked jeera 2 tsp, 2 dalchini small, 3 elaichi, 4 laung, 1/2 tsp black pepper) + 1 green chilli, 12-15 garlic cloves, 1 small piece ginger
  • make onion paste (2 medium)
  • make tomato paste (3 to 4 small)

Cooking

  • heat mustard oil (fish wala) , add 3 elaichi, 1 medium dalchini, 1 tejpata, 4 to 5 laung
  • add 2 sliced onions and fry till golden
  • add onion paste and 1/2 tsp methi, and cook well
  • add the masala paste, salt, bit of haldi sugar little, cook well
  • add tomato paste and cook till oil separates
  • add 50g (very little) dahi and cook well till oil separates
  • add some water to make jhol (not too thin)
  • add the fried fish and potatoes and make sure they submerge and cook till gravy thickens and releases oil on top
  • add little chopped dhaniya, ghee 1 tsp and 1/2 tsp sugar to finish
Background

Kalia is the richer of the Bengali fish curries: more oil, more onion, whole garam masala and a finish of ghee. By convention it belongs to weddings and festivals rather than to daily meals, where a thinner jhol is usual.

The gravy is built in the oil the fish was fried in. Mustard oil that has had fish through it carries that flavour into everything cooked in it afterwards.
