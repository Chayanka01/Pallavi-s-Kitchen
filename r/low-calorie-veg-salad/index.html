Munu special low calorie veg salad

  • Cut peeled cucumber and peeled beetroot into thin matchsticks
  • Thinly slice onion
  • Add to a bowl
  • Add finely chopped tomato, chopped green chillies, chopped garlic and chopped coriander leaves
  • Season with salt, black salt, chaat masala, black pepper powder and a bit of ketchup
  • Mix everything well and serve
