Chicken alu jhol kacha lonka diye

Ginger garlic green chilli Paste

  • Make a paste of 12-15 garlic cloves, half an inch ginger and 7-8 green chillies

Marination

  • Take 500g curry cut chicken and wash it well
  • Add salt, 50g dahi, 1 tsp black pepper powder, 1.5 tsp haldi powder, 2 small sticks of dalchini, 4-5 green elaichi, 5-6 laung, 2 bay leaves, 1 small onion (thinly sliced) , 2 green chillies (broken in half) and above mentioned ginger garlic green chilli paste and 3 tbsp mustard oil
  • Let it marinate for at least half an hour

Potato

  • Peel 2 large potatoes and cut into quarters (if potatoes are small, cut into halves)
  • Boil the potatoes in salted water till fully cooked
  • Drain and wash with cold water and dry fully
  • Fry the boiled potatoes in the 5-6 tbsp mustard oil in the kadhai on medium high (900 induction) till a light golden crispy layer develops on top
  • Remove from oil and keep aside

Garam masala powder

  • Dry roast 3 sticks of dalchini, 7 to 8 elaichi and 15-16 laung
  • Cool down and grind to a fine powder

Curry

  • In the remaining potato frying oil in the kadhai, add 2 small onions (thinly sliced) and fry till it's light brown
  • Add the marinated chicken along with the marinade and cook at medium high (900 induction) till the chicken releases some water
  • Stir everything well and cover with a weighted lid and let everything cook at medium high (900 induction) with stirring in between every 10 minutes
  • Do this process till the chicken has developed some roasted brown spots and is fully cooked on the inside

Note- Splash some water in between if the chicken or masala starts sticking to the kadhai

  • When chicken is fully cooked , add 1.5 cup of boiling hot water
  • Cover and cook for 5 minutes on high heat (1200 induction) and the oil should float to the top and the gravy should be bubbling and a bit reduced
  • Adjust the salt and add the boiled fried potatoes
  • Add 1 tbsp Garam masala powder, 2-3 whole green chillies, 1/2 tsp sugar and 2 tbsp raw mustard oil
  • Cover and turn the heat off immediately.
  • Keep covered for at least 10 minutes before serving
Background

Kacha lonka is green chilli, and here it does the work that dried red chilli does in the Chicken Alu Lal Jhol: seven or eight go into the paste with garlic and ginger, two more into the marinade and the last few whole at the end. The two recipes share the boiled and fried potatoes, the garam masala of dalchini, elaichi and laung, and the finish of raw mustard oil under a closed lid.

This one has no separate paste fried in the oil. The whole spices, onion and green chilli paste all go into the marinade with a little dahi, and the chicken cooks in it under a weighted lid until it browns in spots.
