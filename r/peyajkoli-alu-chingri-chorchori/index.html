Peyajkoli alu diye chingri maach chorchori

Note- Peyajkoli can be substituted by spring onions

Prawns

  • Marinate 200g cleaned medium sized prawns with salt, 1tsp haldi powder and 3-4tbsp mustard oil
  • Heat 5-6 tbsp mustard oil at medium high (900 induction) till the oil is smoking
  • Add the prawns and fry for 3-4 minutes and keep aside
  • Reserve the oil for further cooking

NOTE- Cook the prawns only till they are light pink and slightly curled, do not overcook

Chorchori

  • In the prawn frying oil, add 2 potatoes (small cubes) and fry at medium high (900 induction) till 80% cooked and slightly crispy
  • Push the potatoes to one side of the kadhai and add 1-2 tbsp more mustard oil on the other side
  • Add 1 small onion (finely chopped) and 15-20 cloves of garlic (smashed)
  • Bring heat down to medium (600 induction) and cook for 2 -3 minutes undisturbed
  • On the onion garlic side, add spring onion white part (finely chopped) and let it cook undisturbed for 5 to 6 minutes

Note - If using peyajkoli, add 200g finely chopped peyajkoli at this step

  • Now mix all the vegetables together and add the spring onion greens (finely chopped) and some coriander leaves (finely chopped)

Note- I used around 200g spring onion in total (divided it into whites and greens as whites take longer to cook)

  • Increase heat to medium high (900 induction) and adjust salt
  • Add 1 tsp red chilli powder and mix well and cook for 2 minutes
  • Add the fried prawns, mix and cook for 3-4 minutes
  • Turn off heat, garnish with some fresh coriander leaves and serve
Background

Peyajkoli are the young flower stalks of the onion plant, and spring onion stands in for them here. The whites and greens are split because the whites need longer, so the whites cook first beside the onion and garlic and the greens go in only when everything is mixed together.

Potatoes and aromatics share the kadhai without touching, pushed to opposite sides, each frying at its own pace in the oil left from the prawns. The prawns are fried only until pink and curled and return for the last three or four minutes.
