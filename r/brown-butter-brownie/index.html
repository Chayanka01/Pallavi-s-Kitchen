Brown butter fudgy Brownie

Butter-cocoa mixture

  • In a saucepan melt 70g butter (either salted or unsalted) at very low heat (100/400 induction) till it starts to brown
  • Add 20g dairy whitener and immediately turn off the heat and mix thoroughly
  • In the hot butter mix, add 50g neutral oil, 30g unsweetened cocoa powder, 5g instant coffee powder and 30g chopped milk chocolate and mix well till smooth
  • Keep aside to cool a bit

Egg mixture

  • In a separate pan, add one egg, 1tsp vanilla extract and a pinch of salt and 100g granulated sugar

NOTE - Adjust the amount of salt depending on whether you used salted or unsalted butter

  • Add 30-50 mL water and whisk well till all the sugar is dissolved and the mixture is a bit frothy

Final batter

  • Add the slightly warm butter-cocoa mixture to the egg mixture and mix well
  • Add 75 g maida and fold it in
  • Add 40g chopped milk chocolate chunks and 2 tbsp chopped cashews and fold it in

Baking

  • Grease the baking dish with ghee and line the bottom with a paper brushed with ghee
  • Pre-heat the cooker/kadhai with salt and a utensil stand at medium high (900 induction) for 10-15 minutes
  • Pour the above prepared batter in the lined baking dish
  • Keep the baking dish with the batter on the Pre-heated stand in the cooker/kadhai and close the lid
  • Bake at medium heat(600 induction) for 35-40 minutes
  • Check the centre using a toothpick for doneness and when the centre is just shy of getting fully cooked, turn off the heat
  • Let the brownie finish baking in the hot cooker with heat turned off for 25-30 minutes
  • Take the brownie out and serve
Background

There is no oven here. Like the mawa cakes in the archive, the brownie bakes in a cooker or kadhai heated over a bed of salt, the dish raised on a stand above it, with the lid closed. The heat is switched off while the centre is still slightly underdone, and the brownie finishes in the trapped heat for another half hour.

The butter is browned slowly on the lowest setting, and dairy whitener, a milk powder, goes into it as soon as it colours, adding more of the toasted milk taste. The egg is whisked with the sugar and a little water until the sugar dissolves, which keeps the brownie dense.
