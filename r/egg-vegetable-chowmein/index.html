Egg vegetable chowmein

Chilli oil

  • Heat 7-8 tbsp oil in a pan
  • In a bowl add 9-10 cloves chopped garlic, 1 tbsp chilli powder, 1 tbsp soya sauce , 1 tbsp sugar and 1/2 tsp salt
  • Add 6-7 tbsp hot oil and mix

Noodles

  • Boil 1 pan water on high heat
  • Put half a packet of hakka noodles (DO NOT BREAK IT INTO SMALLER PIECES)
  • Boil for 2 minutes lesser than time instructed on packet
  • Drain and wash immediately by with cold water
  • Transfer in a bowl and mix with all of the chilli oil solids and 3-4 tbsp oil from chilli oil

Cooking

  • In a wide kadhai heat up the remaining 1-2 tbsp chilli oil and 1 tbsp refined oil at high heat (1200 induction)
  • bring heat to medium high (900 induction) and add two eggs, a pinch of salt and 1tsp black pepper powder
  • Scramble the eggs and keep the scramble chunks a bit big
  • Let the eggs get fully cooked and take aside
  • In the same kadhai add 2 more tbsp refined oil and increase heat to high (1200 induction)
  • Add 1 large onion (thinly sliced) and fry for 1 minute
  • Add half a capsicum thinly sliced and fry for 2 minutes
  • Add 8-10 cloves of garlic (finely chopped) and 2 green chillies (finely chopped) and fry for 1 minute
  • Bring heat down to low (400 induction)
  • Add the chilli oil coated noodles
  • Sprinkle 1/2 tsp MSG, 1 tsp meat masala (I used cooked guntur chicken masala but other brands can also be used) and the scrambled eggs
  • Mix everything with the noodles for 1 minute and turn off the flame.
Background

Kolkata is widely regarded as the birthplace of Indian Chinese cooking. Hakka immigrants are recorded in Calcutta from around 1820, and by 1837 the city had 362 Chinese residents. Many Hakka families later settled in Tangra, still known for its Chinese restaurants.

Here the sauce is made first as a chilli oil, hot oil poured over garlic, chilli powder, soy, sugar and salt, and the noodles are coated in it before they reach the kadhai. Boiling them two minutes short of the packet time and rinsing them cold keeps them from going soft once they are tossed.
