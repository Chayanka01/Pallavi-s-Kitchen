Bihari style roasted tomato dhaniya chutney

  • In a tawa heat some mustard oil
  • Put one large tomato thinly sliced into rounds and 10-12 cloves of garlic
  • Roast till the tomato is a bit charred and garlic cloves are lightly browned
  • Cool the roasted tomatoes and roasted garlic and add to a blender jar
  • Add 4-5 cloves of raw garlic, 2-3 green chillies, a handful of chopped dhaniya leaves, juice of one lemon and salt and grind into a fine paste

NOTE- Some water might be added if the chutney is too thick

Background

The tomato is cut into thin rounds so that each slice chars on the tawa in mustard oil, alongside whole garlic cloves that only brown lightly. Raw garlic goes into the blender with the roasted cloves, so the chutney carries both the sharp taste of fresh garlic and the softer one of roasted garlic, under green chilli, dhaniya and lemon.
