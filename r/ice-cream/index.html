Ice cream mixture

  • fresh cream
  • sugar
  • very little cornstarch
  • flavor
  • cook by stirring on induction for 15-20 mins
  • cool and add to small steel bowl
  • Keep the entire setup in freezer
  • Keep stirring vigorously with fork every 30 min - 1 hr
  • After stirring 8-10 times, freeze for 2-3 hr
Background

Salt lowers the freezing point of the ice packed around the inner bowl, so the bath can fall well below 0 °C. Ice cream made before mechanical freezers depended on this, and the arrangement of a small vessel set inside a larger one of ice and salt is very old.

Stirring with a fork every half hour breaks up ice crystals while they are still small. Crystals left undisturbed grow, and large crystals give home-made ice cream its coarse texture. The cornstarch binds some of the free water, leaving less of it available to freeze, which is the job commercial stabilisers do.
