Fulkopi Alu diye Rui macher jhol

Fish

  • Wash and drain 500g rohu (rui) fish pieces
  • Marinate with salt, haldi powder and 2 tbsp mustard oil
  • After marinating for atleast 15 minutes fry the fish in 5-6 tbsp of smoking hot mustard oil in a kadhai
  • Keep the fish pieces aside and reserve the oil

Vegetables

  • Cut half a cauliflower into medium florets
  • Peel and cut one large/2 medium potatoes into long pieces ( 1/4 on the vertical axis)
  • Heat 2-3 tbsp mustard oil in a separate pan (not the fish frying kadhai) at medium (600 induction) and add the cauliflower and potatoes
  • Add 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp haldi powder and fry uncovered for 5-7 minutes
  • Stir in between once and let some browning happen on the vegetables
  • Once some browning has happened, cover the pan with a lid and cook till the vegetables are fully cooked from inside

Note - Stir once in a while to prevent burning

  • Keep them aside

Masala paste

  • Dry roast 4-6 dried red chillies and 1.5 tbsp jeera at medium (600 induction) till they are lightly toasted
  • Cool and grind to a rough powder first
  • Then add 2-3 green chillies and 1 inch ginger knob with the powdered jeera+dried red chillies and make a smooth paste

Curry

  • Heat the fish frying oil in the kadhai at medium (600 induction) and add 1 tsp jeera,1 large tejpatta ,1 dried red chilli, 3 green elaichi, 4-5 laung and 1 inch dalchini stick
  • Fry for 30 seconds and add 1 small onion finely chopped
  • Fry till the onion is slightly brown
  • Add the paste of 2 small/1 medium onion and fry till the paste is brown and the raw smell of onion goes away
  • Add the masala paste and fry well till the smell of raw ginger goes away

NOTE - Splash some water in between if masala starts sticking/burning

  • Add 1 large/2 medium tomatoes (chopped in big chunks) , a splash of water, a pinch of salt and 1/2 tsp sugar
  • Cover and cook at medium (600 induction) for 3-4 minutes or till the tomatoes are soft
  • Open the lid and mix everything well and also smash the tomatoes a bit
  • Add the fried vegetables and mix with the masala
  • Let the vegetables fry with the masala for 5-7 minutes
  • Add 3/4th glass of hot water and increase the heat to high (1200 induction)
  • Cover and cook till oil starts floating on top of the jhol and it has reduced a bit
  • Add the fish pieces, cover and cook at high (1200 induction) for 5 minutes
  • Open the lid, garnish with 1 tbsp ghee, 2 whole green chillies, a pinch of sugar and 1 tsp Sunrise Shahi Garam masala
  • Serve
Background

The cauliflower and potatoes are fried in a separate pan, first uncovered so they brown and then covered until cooked through, so they reach the jhol already cooked and only take on the masala. The fish frying oil carries over into the curry.

The masala is the same pair as in the methi foron jhol, dried red chilli and jeera dry-roasted together, here ground with green chilli and ginger. Onion goes in twice, chopped and then as a paste, and the jhol is finished with ghee and garam masala.
