Egg roast (munuma style)

  • Boil 2 eggs till hard boiled
  • Peel the eggs and make two shallow slits on them lengthwise
  • Add some salt, haldi powder and mirchi powder on the eggs and shallow fry in refined oil till surface is blistered
  • In the same oil add 1/2 tsp jeera, 1 small dalchini, 1 green chilli sliced and paste of two small onions.
  • add some salt and fry till oil separates
  • Add 1 tsp mirchi powder, 1/2 tsp haldi powder and 1/2 tsp jeera powder and saute well.
  • Add 1 tbsp ketchup and mix with the onions.
  • Cook till the ketchup is reduced and oil is separated
  • Add a little water and boil on high till oil floats on top
  • Add the eggs and reduce the gravy a bit more.
  • Serve
Background

In Indian home cooking a roast is a masala cooked down until it clings and the oil separates out, rather than anything done in an oven. The two shallow slits let that masala reach past the white of the egg.

The name is shared with Kerala's nadan mutta roast, which also starts from slit boiled eggs but seasons them with coconut oil, curry leaves and garam masala and is eaten with appam or puttu. Little else lines up. Here the eggs are fried until the surface blisters before they go into the gravy, the whole spices are jeera and dalchini, and the tomato arrives as ketchup, carrying its sugar and vinegar with it.
