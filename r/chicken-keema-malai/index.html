Chicken Keema Malai

Spicemalai
Written19 March 2026
FromHer handwriting
Tagschicken · keema · curry · cream

A. PASTES/POWDER

  1. 1Masala Powder :-
  • Dry Roast - 6-7 loung, 5 green elaichi, 1 inch dalchini, 1 big tejpatta, 12-15 black pepper, 1 big blade javitri, 5-6 dry red chilli
  • Grind into powder
  1. 2Nut paste

Soak 2 tbsp cashew and 1 tbsp kishmish in little water for sometime. Make paste

  1. 3Ginger-Garlic-Green Chilli paste

10-15 garlic cloves 1/2 inch ginger 2 green chillies

  1. 4Onion Paste

1.5 medium sized red onion.

B. COOKING

  1. 1Heat 3-4 tbsp refined oil + 1 tbsp Ghee.
  2. 2Add 1 medium onion chopped + 2 small green chilli chopped.
  3. 3Cook till onions are slightly brown. Add little salt.
  4. 4Add onion paste, Cook till it becomes brownish-pink.
  5. 5Add ginger + garlic + green chilli paste, Cook till raw smell gone
  6. 6Add chopped tomato (1 big). Cook till it is soft and mash it.
  7. 7Increase temp to 900 and add 300g Chicken Keema. Reduce temp to 600.
  8. 8Smash it and cook at 600 temp. till keema releases water.
  9. 9Increase temp. to 900 and fry the keema till it releases fat.
  10. 10Add 1 tsp of prepared masala powder. Mix well
  11. 11Add 200 mL curd and stir vigorously.
  12. 12Keep cooking till water from curd evaporates and keema releases fat again.
  13. 13Add kaju + kishmish paste and mix well. Cook at 900 temp for 2-3 min. Add some (1-2 tsp) Kewra water. Mix well.
  14. 14Turn temperature down to 600 and add 1.5 to 2 tbsp of prepared masala powder, 60-70 mL cream, salt and two sliced green chillies. Mix well. Add 15-20 kishmish.
  15. 15Turn temp to 1200. Let keema roast for 1-2 min.
  16. 16Add chopped dhaniya patta, kasuri methi. Mix well and serve.

INGREDIENTS

  1. 1Chicken Keema - 300g
  2. 2Onion - 3 medium └ 2 for paste, 1 for chopping
  3. 3Garlic - 10-15 cloves
  4. 4Ginger - 1/2 inch
  5. 5Green chillies - 6 └ 2 for paste, 2 chopped, 2 sliced
  6. 6Tomato - 1 large
  7. 7Kaju (cashew) - 2 tbsp
  8. 8Kishmish - 2 tbsp (1 tbsp for paste, 1 tbsp for later)
  9. 9Curd - 200g
  10. 10Fresh Cream - 70 mL
  11. 11Coriander leaves
  12. 12Kasuri methi
  13. 13Kewra Water
  14. 14Salt
  15. 15Refined oil
  16. 16Ghee
  17. 17Whole spices :- i) Green cardamom (hari elaichi) - 5 ii) Clove (loung) - 6-7 iii) Cinnamon (dalchini) - 1 inch iv) Bay leaves (tejpatta) - 1 large v) Black Pepper (kalimirch) - 12-15 vi) Mace (Javitri) - 1 big blade vii) Dried Red Chillies - 5-6
Background

Cream, kewra water, mace in the ground masala and kasuri methi crumbled in at the close place this closer to north Indian restaurant cooking than to an eastern kitchen. Kewra is distilled from screwpine flowers and used in small quantities, mostly in sweets and in rich meat dishes.

The keema is fried until it releases fat, curd is added, and it is cooked again until fat separates a second time. Passing through that stage twice gives a dry keema rather than a wet one.
