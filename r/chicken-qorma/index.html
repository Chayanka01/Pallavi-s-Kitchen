Chicken Qorma (Korma)

Fried Onion Paste

  • Thinly slice 4 medium sized onions
  • Deep fry in hot oil till golden brown
  • Strain out from oil and drain on a paper towel to remove excess oil
  • Grind 90% of the fried onions WITHOUT ADDING EXTRA WATER into a paste
  • Reserve the remaining 10% for garnish

Homemade Garam Masala powder

  • Dry roast 8-9 dried red chillies, 2 tbsp coriander seeds, 1 tbsp jeera seeds, 2 sticks of dalchini, 1 tsp kababchini, 1.5 tsp black pepper, 1 big blade of javitri, 6-7 green elaichi, 1 black elaichi (only seeds), 9-10 laung and 1/4th nutmeg grated
  • Cool and grind to a fine powder

Marination

  • Wash and clean 500g curry cut chicken
  • Add 2 tsp salt, 2 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder, 1 tsp red chilli powder, 1/2 tsp haldi powder, 1 tsp kewra water, 200g curd, ginger garlic paste (1 inch cube ginger and 20-25 garlic cloves) , homemade garam masala powder (1.5 -2 tbsp) and 1 tbsp ghee to the chicken
  • Mix well and let it marinate for at least half an hour

Cooking

  • In a kadhai add 4-5 tbsp of onion frying oil and heat it up at medium high (900 induction)
  • Add 3 green elaichi, 1 black elaichi (only seeds), 1 dalchini stick and let it fry for 1-2 minutes
  • Add the marinated chicken and cook at high (1200 induction) , UNCOVERED for 8-10 minutes.
  • Stir once or twice in between
  • Bring heat down to medium high (900 induction) and cover tightly with a lid and let it cook for 25-30 minutes till the oil starts floating on top

NOTE- Stir every 7-10 minutes to avoid sticking

  • When the curd is fully cooked and reduced and a lot of oil is released on top add ~200 mL hot water
  • Add the Fried onion paste and mix well
  • Cover and let it cook for 10-15 minutes till the oil separates again and the gravy is thickened to your liking
  • Bring the heat down to low (400 induction)
  • Add 1 tbsp Homemade garam masala, the 10% reserved fried onions, 1 tbsp ghee, 2 drops of meetha attar, 1 tsp kewra water and 1/2 tsp lazzat-e-taam masala
  • Mix, cover and turn off flame

* Before serving, garnish with sliced green chillies, julienned ginger and lemon juice

Background

The gravy depends on two things made in advance: onions deep-fried to golden brown and ground without water, and a garam masala roasted at home with kababchini, javitri, black elaichi and nutmeg. The chicken first cooks in its curd marinade, uncovered on high heat and then covered, until the curd has reduced and the oil comes away. The onion paste and hot water go in only after that.

The finish uses kewra water, meetha attar and lazzat-e-taam. All three belong to Lucknow cooking, where meetha attar, kewra and rose water flavour the biryani. Lazzat-e-taam is a ground spice blend from Awadh, the powdered form of potli masala, and kewra and meetha attar are themselves among its ingredients.
