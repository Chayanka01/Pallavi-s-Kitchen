Special Masala Powder

Dry roast

  • Dalchini 2 sticks
  • Green elaichi 5 to 6
  • Laung 8
  • Javitri 1.5 big blade
  • Kabab chini 1/2 tsp
  • Black pepper 1 tsp
  • Radhuni 1/4 tsp

Cool down and grind to make powder

Masala paste

  • Soak 7 to 8 dried red chillies in boiling water
  • Make a paste with 1.5 inch ginger knob and 30-35 garlic cloves

Marinade

  • Wash and drain mutton pieces (500g).
  • Add 100g dahi, juice of 1 lemon, salt, 1tsp haldi powder, 2 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder, half of the masala paste (ginger, garlic, dried red chilli), 2 tsp special masala Powder, 2tbsp mustard oil. Mix well with mutton and marinate for atleast 30min

Potato

  • Cut potatoes into big chunks.
  • Heat mustard oil (5-6tbsp) in a kadhai at medium heat (600 induction).
  • Add the potatoes and fry for 5 min
  • Add 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp haldi powder and 1tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder directly. Mix with potatoes and fry on medium heat (600 induction) till potatoes are 70% done.
  • Take potatoes out and keep aside.

Gravy

  • In the potato frying oil, add 1 inch dalchini stick, 2 small bay leaves, 5 laung and 3 green elaichi
  • Add 4 medium onions thinly sliced and fry on medium high heat (900 induction) till onion is slightly brown.
  • Add remaining half of the masala paste (ginger, garlic, dried red chilli) and fry till oil releases.
  • Add mutton with marinade and cook on high heat (1200 induction) for 5 min
  • Lower heat to medium high (900 induction) , cover and cook for 15-20 min or till mutton releases water and some fat floats on top.
  • Add ~1L boiling hot water to the mutton, cover and cook at 1000-1200 induction heat till mutton is cooked almost completely and water is reduced (STIR IN BETWEEN)
  • Lower heat to medium (600 induction) and scrape the sides and bottom to incorporate the brown bits, add very little boiling water and cover for 4 to 5 min (do this 3 to 4 times to get a dark brown gravy)
  • Add 500 mL boiling water, two whole green chillies, grated nutmeg (1 small piece) and the potatoes.
  • Cover and cook at medium high (900 induction) till potatoes are well cooked and gravy reduced to liking.
  • lower heat to low (400 induction) and add 1 tsp sugar, 1 tbsp ghee and 1 tbsp special masala powder
  • Mix and cover, cook at low for 2 min , turn off heat and serve.
Background

Kosha describes a method rather than an ingredient. The masala is fried down with very little liquid, water added a spoonful at a time, and the browned residue left on the pan is scraped back in at each stage. Four or five repetitions produce the dark gravy.

Two spices in the ground powder are uncommon. Radhuni grows across South and Southeast Asia but is used as a spice mainly in Bengali cooking, and is frequently sold and mistaken as celery seed. Kabab chini is cubeb pepper, widely used in Europe in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries and largely abandoned there once black pepper became cheap.
