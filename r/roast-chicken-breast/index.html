Roast chicken breast recipe

  • roasted masala paste- 15 -20 garlic cloves, dried red chilli 4 to 5, black pepper 1tsp, dry roast, grind and then add water to make paste
  • marinate chicken breast with dahi, salt, vinegar, garlic powder, soya sauce, roasted masala paste
  • fry some onions in refined oil and take out of kadhai and keep aside when brown
  • in the same oil add paste of 1 large onion and 1tsp kashmiri chilli powder
  • cook till onion paste fried well
  • add chicken + marinade and some dhaniya leaves
  • cook on 900 for 5 minutes without cover and then cover and cook on 600 for 10 minutes
  • flip the pieces, if it's sticking add some dahi to deglaze the pan ( DON'T ADD WATER)
  • cover and cook again at 600 for 10-15 minutes, it'll be roasted well
  • open the cover, add the fried onion, some more dhaniya, small pinch of sugar and few dollops of dahi for garnish
  • done
Background

This is not a traditional dish. Curd and vinegar in the marinade, soya sauce, and a dry-roasted paste of garlic, red chilli and black pepper standing in for a spice mix make it a modern household construction.

Deglazing with curd rather than water keeps the pan juices concentrated, so the chicken carries on roasting instead of stewing in the liquid it releases.
