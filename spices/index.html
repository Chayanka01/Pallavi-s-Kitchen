  • হলুদholud

    Turmeric · Curcuma longa

    The dried, ground rhizome of a plant in the ginger family. It goes in with the salt near the start, and is rubbed onto fish with salt before frying.

    In 16 recipesChicken Alu Lal Jhol, Chicken Champaran Kosha, Chicken Qorma, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha, Echor Chingri, Kathal Chingri, Macher Kalia, Methi Foron Diye Alu Rui Macher Jhol, Palong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori, Peyajkoli Alu Diye Chingri Maach Chorchori, Pumpkin Fish Curry, Egg Roast, Barojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Sheem Alu Mishtikumro Posto, Alu Shingara, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • শুকনো লঙ্কাshukno lonka

    Dried red chilli · Capsicum annuum

    Whole chillies dried in the sun. They are fried whole in the first oil, soaked and ground into paste, or dry-roasted and ground with other seeds.

    In 13 recipesChicken Alu Lal Jhol, Chicken Champaran Kosha, Chicken Keema Malai, Chicken Qorma, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha, Roast Chicken Breast, Macher Kalia, Methi Foron Diye Alu Rui Macher Jhol, Palong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori, Pumpkin Fish Curry, Barojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Sheem Alu Mishtikumro Posto, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • সর্ষেsorshe

    Mustard · Brassica juncea

    Mustard oil, pressed from the seed, is the cooking fat in most of these recipes. A spoonful of it raw often finishes the dish.

    In 13 recipesChicken Alu Lal Jhol, Chicken Champaran Kosha, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha, Echor Chingri, Kathal Chingri, Macher Kalia, Methi Foron Diye Alu Rui Macher Jhol, Palong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori, Peyajkoli Alu Diye Chingri Maach Chorchori, Pumpkin Fish Curry, Barojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Sheem Alu Mishtikumro Posto, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • পাঁচফোড়নpanch phoron

    Five-seed mix

    Five whole seeds together: fennel, cumin, nigella, fenugreek, and radhuni or black mustard, as the labra recipe lists them. Fried in oil before anything else goes in.

    In 3 recipesPalong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori, Barojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • রাঁধুনিradhuni

    Radhuni · Trachyspermum roxburghianum

    A small seed from a relative of celery and ajwain, used almost only in Bengal. Some households use it as the fifth seed of panch phoron. Source

    In 3 recipesMutton Alu Jhol / Kosha, Barojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • মৌরিmouri

    Fennel seed · Foeniculum vulgare

    One of the five seeds of panch phoron. In the labra it is dry-roasted with coriander and radhuni for the bhaja moshla.

    In 2 recipesBarojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • কালো জিরেkalo jeere

    Nigella · Nigella sativa

    A small, matt black seed, also sold as kalonji. It goes into hot oil at the very start.

    In 4 recipesBarojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Sheem Alu Mishtikumro Posto, Alu Shingara, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • মেথিmethi

    Fenugreek seed · Trigonella foenum-graecum

    A hard, bitter seed used in small amounts. In the methi foron jhol it is the first thing in the oil and gives the dish its name.

    In 6 recipesMacher Kalia, Methi Foron Diye Alu Rui Macher Jhol, Pumpkin Fish Curry, Barojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Sheem Alu Mishtikumro Posto, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • জিরেjeera

    Cumin · Cuminum cyminum

    Used whole in tempering, ground to jeera powder, and soaked and ground into the paste for macher kalia.

    In 11 recipesChicken Qorma, Echor Chingri, Kathal Chingri, Macher Kalia, Methi Foron Diye Alu Rui Macher Jhol, Palong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori, Pumpkin Fish Curry, Egg Roast, Barojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Alu Shingara, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • তেজপাতাtejpatta

    Indian bay leaf · Cinnamomum tamala

    The leaf of a cinnamon relative, different from the Mediterranean bay. It goes into the oil whole with the other whole spices.

    In 10 recipesChicken Alu Lal Jhol, Chicken Keema Malai, Echor Chingri, Kathal Chingri, Macher Kalia, Methi Foron Diye Alu Rui Macher Jhol, Palong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori, Barojibi (Barui) Special Labra Torkari, Chirer Pulao, Masur Dal Panch Foron Diye

  • দারচিনিdalchini

    Cinnamon · Cinnamomum

    Bark, broken into short pieces. Used whole in the oil, or roasted and ground with elaichi and laung for garam masala.

    In 11 recipesChicken Alu Lal Jhol, Chicken Champaran Kosha, Chicken Keema Malai, Chicken Qorma, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha, Echor Chingri, Kathal Chingri, Macher Kalia, Palong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori, Egg Roast, Chirer Pulao

  • এলাচelaichi

    Green cardamom · Elettaria cardamomum

    Green pods, used whole in the oil for meat and fish, ground into garam masala, and dropped into the syrup for rosogolla.

    In 10 recipesChicken Alu Lal Jhol, Chicken Champaran Kosha, Chicken Keema Malai, Chicken Qorma, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha, Echor Chingri, Kathal Chingri, Macher Kalia, Palong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori, Rosogolla

  • লবঙ্গlaung

    Clove · Syzygium aromaticum

    The dried flower bud of a tree. In these recipes it appears with dalchini and elaichi.

    In 7 recipesChicken Alu Lal Jhol, Chicken Champaran Kosha, Chicken Qorma, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha, Kathal Chingri, Macher Kalia, Palong Shaak Diye Macher Mathar Chorchori

  • কাবাবচিনিkabab chini

    Cubeb · Piper cubeba

    The dried berry of a pepper vine, sold with a short stalk still attached. Roasted and ground into the masala paste.

    In 4 recipesChicken Champaran Kosha, Chicken Qorma, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha, Kathal Chingri

  • জয়িত্রীjavitri

    Mace · Myristica fragrans

    The red, lacy covering around the nutmeg seed, dried. A single small blade goes into the dry-roasted masala.

    In 4 recipesChicken Champaran Kosha, Chicken Keema Malai, Chicken Qorma, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha

  • জায়ফলjaiphal

    Nutmeg · Myristica fragrans

    The seed inside the mace, grated straight into the marinade.

    In 3 recipesChicken Champaran Kosha, Chicken Qorma, Mutton Alu Jhol / Kosha

  • পোস্তposto

    Poppy seed · Papaver somniferum

    Tiny cream-coloured seeds, soaked and ground with chillies into a thick paste.

    In 1 recipeSheem Alu Mishtikumro Posto

  • বড় এলাচboro elach

    Black cardamom · Amomum subulatum

    Large, wrinkled pods dried over fire, which gives them a smoky smell. In the qorma only the seeds are used, both in the garam masala and in the first oil.

    In 1 recipeChicken Qorma

  • জোয়ানajwain

    Carom seed · Trachyspermum ammi

    Small ridged seeds that smell of thyme. Half a teaspoon goes into the shingara dough with the kalonji.

    In 1 recipeAlu Shingara

  • হিংhing

    Asafoetida · Ferula assa-foetida

    Dried resin from the root of a giant fennel, usually sold ground and cut with flour. It goes into hot oil with the jeera at the start of the shingara filling.

    In 1 recipeAlu Shingara

  • আমচুরamchur

    Dried mango powder · Mangifera indica

    Unripe mango, sliced, dried in the sun and ground. It sours the shingara filling without adding liquid.

    In 1 recipeAlu Shingara

  • কসুরি মেথিkasuri methi

    Dried fenugreek leaves · Trigonella foenum-graecum

    The leaves of the same plant whose seeds are methi, dried and crumbled. A tablespoon goes into the shingara filling.

    In 2 recipesChicken Keema Malai, Alu Shingara

  • কেওড়াkewra

    Kewra water · Pandanus odoratissimus

    Water distilled from the scented flowers of a screwpine. In the qorma a teaspoon goes into the marinade and another at the end.

    In 2 recipesChicken Keema Malai, Chicken Qorma

  • মিঠা আতরmeetha attar

    Meetha attar

    A sweet food-grade perfume used in Awadhi, old Delhi and Mughlai cooking. Most of what is sold now is a blended biryani essence. Two drops finish the qorma. Source

    In 1 recipeChicken Qorma

  • লজ্জত-এ-তামlazzat-e-taam

    Lazzat-e-taam

    A ground spice blend from Awadh, the powdered form of potli masala. Rose petals, sandalwood, vetiver, mace, cubeb, kewra and meetha attar are among its many parts. Half a teaspoon finishes the qorma. Source

    In 1 recipeChicken Qorma
